What is Bandcast?

Bandcast is built to help friends and family communicate with ease.

Why Bandcast?

Bandcast offers a more user-friendly platform compared to Discord, Signal, and even iMessage. This allows people to concentrate on communication without difficulty.

How it works

Invite your friends and family members to become part of the Bandcast community.

Who it's for

Bandcast is for friends and family who want a straightforward way to talk and chat privately. You must be 13 or older to use Bandcast.

Privacy

Your conversations will remain private. Bandcast will be developed to support secure messaging for you to enjoy private conversations.
